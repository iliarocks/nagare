Privacy

Nagare stores your tasks, projects, notes, schedules, and repeat settings on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, this data is stored in your private iCloud database and synchronized by Apple. Nagare does not send this data to the developer and does not use advertising or analytics services.

You can delete your data by deleting Nagare and disabling iCloud sync in Settings. If you contact support, your email address and message are used only to respond to you. For privacy questions, contact support@nagare.page.
